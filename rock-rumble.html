Rock Rumble

You’re the gold rock. You see through your rock’s eyes. Move the mouse to look, WASD to move, dash into rivals, and knock them into the lava. Last rock standing wins. Every map is different. Grab the glowing gems for Mega Rock or Quake. Earn 5 points per KO and 10 per win, then spend them in the Shop. Every rock has 100 HP: each dash hit costs 1 HP, and 0 HP or the lava knocks you out.

Keys: WASD or arrows to move, mouse to look (Esc frees the mouse), space to dash, then S right after a dash to brake. On touch: drag the left side to move and the right side to look.

Music: “game lobby music” by cursewurm, on YouTube. Press M to mute.
